## Review method

I opened the current generated local pages in Chrome at a 1200 × 900 desktop viewport. Pages inspected: research index, programme map, Chapter 1, one-page summary, sources and Chapter 10. I also checked Chapter 10's diagram and cost table in the site's dark theme, alongside the default light theme, and inspected the research source/method text. Final acceptance applies to the local working tree; deployment was not part of this pass.

For the research claims, I checked the programme's own source ledger against primary pages for the Chroma Context Rot report and the cited 2026 agent-context and retrieval papers. Chroma explicitly describes controlled retrieval and question-answer tasks and says those tests do not reproduce the full complexity of agent tasks. That supports a careful context-length synthesis; it does not directly establish the programme's headline as a coding-agent result. See [Chroma's report](https://www.trychroma.com/research/context-rot), [TRACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06503), [the agentic-search paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15184), and [the observation-masking paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.21433).

Follow-up: the wide-rail controls were reviewed in Chrome at 1368 × 900 and 1440 × 900 CSS viewport sizes. Both open rails, each rail collapsed alone, and both collapsed were inspected; Enter and Space toggled the native disclosures, and the focused control retained its visible outline. The 1440 screenshot capture was physically 1200 px wide, so I inspected the right rail in a browser screenshot crop and used live layout measurements for the complete 1440 px grid.

## Root-cause synthesis

- **Reading hierarchy:** the page template gave navigation and two chapter summaries prominent space before the argument. F01 addresses the rails; F06 is closed with a compact visible brief and collapsed learning outcomes.
- **Evidence scope drift:** headline copy, synthesis claims and recommendations were copied across surfaces without carrying each source's task, population and conditions with them. Evidence labels alone do not show whether a paper directly tested the claim. F02, F03, F09–F12 now carry the relevant scope limits onto the page.
- **Reader entry:** the index explained the service before presenting the only published programme as an action. F05 is closed with a direct hero CTA above the generic promises.
- **Method transparency:** the method page summarized an internal completion score without criterion-level evidence or a reproducible search method. F07 now labels the score and research design's limits directly.
- **Metadata consistency:** the programme map separates main chapters, appendices and its reference category, but the research index used raw array lengths. F14 now uses shared category-aware counts on both surfaces.
- **Progressive navigation:** F15 lets readers close either wide-screen rail without losing the other or its wayfinding content; collapsed controls preserve access and the reading column stays within its comfortable maximum.
