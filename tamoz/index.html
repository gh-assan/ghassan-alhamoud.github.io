What would you like to understand better?

You may already have readings and alarms, but still spend time working out what they mean and what to do next. Or you may need sensors before you can see what is happening.

  • Energy consumption that stays high when equipment is idle.
  • Changes in a process that may be contributing to material waste.
  • Recurring alerts that take too long to investigate.

We start with one problem your team wants to investigate and a result we can measure.

An example: energy use while a line is idle

If energy use stays high while a production line is idle, we could compare consumption with operating schedules and investigate which equipment contributes. The team could review the findings, decide whether to change anything, and measure consumption afterwards.

This is a possible pilot, not a customer result. It would test whether the information helps the team find and reduce avoidable consumption.

What Tamoz brings to the pilot

Tamoz is the software we are building to connect monitoring with investigation and response. It follows sensor readings, flags changes, and uses AI to bring relevant information together and suggest what to check or do. Your team reviews the recommendations and decides what happens next.

What we would do together

  1. Define the question. Choose one asset or process, agree on the scope, and decide how to measure the results.
  2. Establish the data. Assess existing readings and operating records. Where suitable data is missing, we can install sensors as part of the agreed pilot, following your site requirements.
  3. Monitor and investigate. Review available history or collect a baseline. Test alerts and recommendations with your team on a read-only feed.
  4. Review the results. Compare the findings with the agreed measures. Decide whether there is a useful next step.

You would get the recorded findings, recommendations with their supporting evidence, and a review of what worked and what needs more investigation. The measures depend on the problem: energy use, material waste, time spent reviewing alerts, or usefulness of recommendations.

Your team stays in control. Any later equipment action would need a separate agreement.
